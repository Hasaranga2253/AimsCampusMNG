# Use Cases

## Overview
AIMS is intended to support the main academic and administrative workflows of a campus management system.

## Student
Students will need access to dashboards, course information, assignments, attendance, and results.

## Lecturer
Lecturers will need access to teaching modules, attendance workflows, assignment handling, and results-related operations.

## Internal Moderator
Internal moderators will need support for reviews, second marking, and approval-related academic quality workflows.

## Programme Coordinator
Programme coordinators will need visibility into programmes, delivery planning, and reporting.

## Administrator
Administrators will need control over users, programmes, modules, timetables, and system-level management activities.
